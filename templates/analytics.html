Population Analytics & Demographic Exploration

Cohort-level distributions, statistical disparities, and readmission incidence across the 101,766 encounters.

3-Class & Binary Target Distribution
Key Insight: 30-day readmissions account for 11.16% (11,357 encounters), while 88.84% are non-readmissions or readmissions after 30 days. Baseline majority-class accuracy is 88.84%.
Demographic Stratification (Age, Gender, Race)
Key Insight: Readmission rates increase with age (from 4.0% in pediatric cases to 12.4% in 70-80 age bracket). Gender shows no statistically significant association (χ2 = 1.24, p = 0.539).
Prior Healthcare Utilization (Inpatient, Emergency, Outpatient)
Dominant Predictive Signal: Mean prior inpatient admissions for readmitted patients (1.22 visits) is more than double non-readmitted patients (0.56 visits, p < 10-300). Patients with ≥3 prior admissions face >27% readmission risk.
Clinical Encounter Predictors
Clinical Metrics: Longer hospital stays (mean 4.77 vs 4.35 days), polypharmacy (mean 16.9 vs 15.9 medications), and higher comorbidity count (mean 7.69 vs 7.39 diagnoses) are all statistically significantly associated with 30-day readmissions.
Glycemic Testing & Medication Titration
Testing & Titration: Active insulin dosage adjustments (Up/Down) correlate with higher readmission rates (12.5% vs 10.1% without insulin). HbA1c testing is strongly informative of physician order intent.
Missingness & Data Integrity Audit
Missingness Strategy: `weight` was excluded (>96.8% missing). Medical specialty (49.1%) and payer code (39.6%) are preserved as informative 'Missing/Unknown' categories.
